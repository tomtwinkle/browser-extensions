# Experiments

## QR caption transport

QR is a post-core proof of concept. It must not be a normal dependency. Test frame rate, payload size, correction codes, sequence gaps, reordering, expiry, corruption, and privacy. Do not encode participant identity, meeting URLs, secrets, or unapproved text. A receiving extension can recover only what the shared image actually carries; this is not end-to-end confidentiality from Meet or screen-sharing infrastructure.

## Acoustic transport

No audible or ultrasonic signal is generated by normal operation. A future experiment must separately measure mic and tab paths, codec/noise suppression, retained bandwidth above 20 kHz, audibility, packet error rate, effective bytes per second, latency, and impact on speech recognition. “Inaudible” does not mean encrypted or undetectable.

## P2P and speaker inference

WebRTC DataChannel and signaling are separate requirements. Do not assume a Meet URL or extension messaging discovers another computer. Do not configure a public STUN/TURN service without approval. Diarization may estimate speaker regions; it cannot establish word correctness, separate overlapping voices, or assign a speaker number to a person's name.

## ASR/runtime and translation candidates

Candidate comparisons stay offline and use one ASR, one translator, and one changed axis per round. Runtime alternatives replace the currently selected backend; they are not additional always-on recognizers. Current model/runtime candidate states and restart conditions live in `research/candidates.json`.
